How to play

First move — click any empty square to claim it. It starts with 3 dots.

Each turn — click one of your own squares to add 1 dot.

Explosions — when a square reaches 4 dots, it explodes! It sends one dot to each neighbouring square (up, down, left, right). Dots only go where neighbours exist — nothing falls off the board.

Capturing — when your dot lands on an opponent's square, you take it over. All the dots become yours.

Chain reactions — if a captured square now has 4+ dots, it explodes too. This can cascade across the whole board in one turn!

Elimination — lose all your squares and you're out.

Winning — last player standing wins.
